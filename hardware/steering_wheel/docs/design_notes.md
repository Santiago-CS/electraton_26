# IRT steering wheel controller: design notes (rev 0.2)

The schematic is generated by `steering_wheel.py` (SKiDL 2.3). **Edit the script and re-run it; don't edit the `.kicad_sch` files.** Anything you change directly in Eeschema is lost on the next run.

```
cd ~/projects/irt/steering_wheel
source .venv/bin/activate
cd pcb
python steering_wheel.py          # netlist + KiCad sheets + bom.csv
python tools/check_sch.py         # re-reads the sheets, checks every pin against the netlist
python tools/check_sch.py --render   # also writes build/*.png previews
```

Open `steering_wheel.kicad_pro` in KiCad 10. The files are written in KiCad 9 format, which KiCad 10 opens natively; save once in KiCad 10 to upgrade them.

The board (`steering_wheel.kicad_pcb`) is generated from the same netlist by `tools/layout_steering.py`. Since rev 0.2 it is **placed on the mechanical outline (`docs/PCB_Volante_2026.DXF`) but not routed yet** (section 6). If you edit the schematic afterwards, use *Update PCB from Schematic* in KiCad, or re-run the placement.

**Rev 0.2 (2026-09-30):** the on-board EC11 encoders, the two PTS645 buttons and the BTN_EXT header are replaced by JST PH connectors for off-board controls: 2 encoders (4-pin), 8 active buttons (4-pin, on a TCA9555 I/O expander) and 2 passive buttons (2-pin). The LCD socket J501 is now a 14-pin JST PH cable connector. The board outline, mounting holes and placement follow the DXF. The schematic review is in section 10.

## 1. Block diagram

```

  │ D+/D-                          │  BAT                                  │
  └──────────► RW612 USB           └─► 1S Li-ion 4000 mAh (J103)          SW101 ─► Q102 P-FET
                                        DW01A + FS8205A protection          │
                                        MAX17048 gauge ── PM I2C ─► RW612   VSYS_SW
                                                                             ├─► TPS63001 ─► +3V3 (RW612, flash, PSRAM, pull-ups)
                                                                             └─► TPS61023 ─► +5V  (LCD, 9x WS2812B)  [BOOST_EN]
RW612ET (TFBGA145) ── FlexSPI A: W25Q512JV 64 MB flash │ FlexSPI B: APS6404L 8 MB PSRAM
                   ── LCDIC SPI ─► 4.0" ST7796S LCD (J501) │ FC2 I2C ─► FT6336U touch
```

## 2. Decisions

| Topic | Choice |
|---|---|
| MCU | RW612ETA2I, the same TFBGA145 package as the FRDM-RW612. Ball map extracted from `docs/FRDM-RW612-SCH.pdf` (all 145 balls), and the supply/RF/strap circuit copied from the FRDM. |
| Display | LCDwiki MSP4031 (ST7796S + FT6336U) on the FRDM LCD pins (LCDIC). SD-card pins (SD_CS, MISO) are not connected. Connected by **cable**: J501 is a 14-pin JST PH header (B14B-PH-K-S) wired 1:1 to the module's 14-pin header. |
| Charger | BQ24074 with power path, so the system runs from USB while the battery charges and charge termination stays clean. |
| Charge status | MAX17048 (state of charge in %, voltage, charge rate, alert interrupt) plus the BQ24074 ~CHG / ~PGOOD pins to GPIOs. |
| Battery | 1S 3.7 V 4000 mAh. Charging at 1.0 A takes about 4.5 h. The safety timer is set to about 9 h. |
| NeoPixels | 9x WS2812B: up to 0.55 A at full white, fed from the 5 V boost. |
| Encoders | 2x Alps EC11 with push switch, **off-board** on JST PH 4-pin (A, B, SW, GND). Pull-ups and Alps RC filters stay on the board, same GPIOs as before. |
| Buttons | 8 **active** buttons on JST PH 4-pin (VCC, in, out, GND): *in* = MCU → button (e.g. its LED), *out* = button → MCU. All 16 lines go to one TCA9555 on the PM I2C bus, with its ~INT on GPIO63. VCC is +3.3 V through a 0.2 A resettable fuse (F501). 2 **passive** buttons (switch to GND) on JST PH 2-pin, on GPIO11/25 as before. |
| Control connectors | JST PH 2.0 mm vertical through-hole (B4B-/B2B-/B14B-PH-K-S). The controls sit in the side wings and the LCD cable in the strip above the display, all outside the LCD, so cables can be plugged with the display fitted. |
| Antenna | **U.FL only** (default while you decide). Pi-match pads C413/C415/L403 are unpopulated for tuning. |
| KiCad | 10 (files written in KiCad 9 format). |

These changes were made during the build and were not in the plan:

- **Separate "PM" I2C bus** on FC0 (GPIO0 = SDA, GPIO5 = SCL) for the fuel gauge and the expansion header. The touch controller is powered by the LCD's 5 V rail. If it shared the gauge's bus, an unpowered LCD would clamp that bus. Now the battery can be read with the display off.
- **1 kΩ in series with RW612 USB_VBUS.** With USB plugged in and the power switch OFF, the RW612 is unpowered while VBUS is live, and the resistor limits the current into that pin.

## 3. Power

| Rail | Source | Load estimate |
|---|---|---|
| VBUS 5 V | USB-C, 5.1 k Rd on CC1/CC2 (default USB power / Type-C current) | charger input, limited to 1.42 A |
| VSYS | BQ24074 OUT: 4.4 V regulated with USB, VBAT without | everything |
| VSYS_SW | VSYS through Q102 (AO3401A) when SW101 is ON | regulators |
| +3V3 | TPS63001 buck-boost (1.2 A buck / 0.8 A boost at Vin > 2.4 V) | RW612 ~0.35–0.45 A peak (Wi-Fi TX), flash/PSRAM ~50 mA, misc ~20 mA, active buttons ≤ 0.2 A (F501 hold) |
| +5V | TPS61023 boost, ~1.5 A capable, true disconnect when disabled | NeoPixels ≤0.55 A + LCD ~0.1–0.15 A |
| +1V8 / VCORE | RW612 internal bucks with L201/L202 (1 µH) | internal |

Charger settings: ISET R104 887 Ω → 1.0 A (590 Ω → 1.5 A, runs hotter). ILIM R105 1.13 kΩ → 1.42 A. TMR R106 68 kΩ → about 9.1 h fast-charge timer (pre-charge about 0.9 h). ITERM is left open (internal default). TS uses a 10 kΩ bias (R107). To use a pack NTC, remove R107 and fit J102.

With the switch OFF, the battery still charges from USB and the fuel gauge keeps running (about 25 µA). The other drains are DW01A (about 3 µA), Q102 leakage and, until it is fixed, the charge-LED path into the unpowered MCU (section 10, should-fix 1).

## 4. Firmware hooks

| Signal | GPIO | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| BOOST_EN | 58 | High = 5 V on (LCD + NeoPixels). A 100 k pull-down keeps it off at reset. **Keep it high while driving LCD/touch pins.** |
| MCU_CHG_STAT_N | 55 | BQ24074 ~CHG. Low = charging. |
| MCU_CHG_PG_N | 56 | BQ24074 ~PGOOD. Low = USB power present. |
| FG_ALRT_N | 57 | MAX17048 ~ALRT (low SOC, 1 % SOC change, voltage alerts). Open drain with 10 k pull-up. |
| PM I2C | 0 (SDA) / 5 (SCL) | FC0 I2C. MAX17048 at 0x36: VCELL reg 0x02 (78.125 µV/LSB), SOC reg 0x04 (1/256 %), CRATE reg 0x16 (0.208 %/h), CONFIG reg 0x0C. TCA9555 at 0x20. |
| BTN_INT_N | 63 | TCA9555 ~INT, open drain with 10 k pull-up (R529). Goes low when any *out* input changes; reading input port 1 clears it. |

**Active buttons (TCA9555, 0x20).** Button *k* (1–8, connector J506+*k*): *in* = P0.(k−1), an output; *out* = P1.(k−1), an input. Registers: input ports 0x00/0x01, output ports 0x02/0x03, polarity 0x04/0x05, configuration 0x06/0x07 (1 = input). At power-up all 16 pins are inputs with 100 k pull-ups, so write 0x02 (levels) and then 0x06 = 0x00 early in boot to make port 0 outputs. An unconnected *out* reads high.

**"Fully charged"** = PGOOD low **and** CHG high (charger terminated). Confirm with SOC ≥ 99 % from the MAX17048. **"Charging"** = PGOOD low and CHG low. **"On battery"** = PGOOD high.

**Control connectors** (JST PH, pin 1 marked on the silkscreen):

| Ref | Function | Pin 1 | Pin 2 | Pin 3 | Pin 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| J503, J504 | Encoder 1, 2 | A | B | SW | GND (common C and switch return) |

Straps are the same as the FRDM. RF_CNTL2 has a 4.7 k pull-down, so the debug port is SWD. RF_CNTL0 is left to its internal default (40 MHz crystal). CONFIG_HOST_BOOT[3:0] = 1111 boots from flash. Hold **ISP** (EXT_REQ → 0) while pressing **RESET** for ISP boot over UART/USB.

## 6. Layout, 8-layer variant (rev 0.2)

**Outline.** Edge.Cuts and the four holes come from `docs/PCB_Volante_2026.DXF` (SolidWorks export, read by `tools/dxf_outline.py`):
- Arcs and lines are copied exactly. The splines are flattened to 5 µm segments.
- The outline is 138.9 × 83.8 mm and mirror-symmetric, with a small bump at top and bottom centre. The side "ears" carry the holes.
- The four holes are **3.0 mm** as drawn, plain NPTH. The top two are only 1.3 mm from the edge, so a plated ring would run off the board.
- Placement keeps 3.5 mm free around each hole for screw heads.

**Layers and stackup.** This board is the **8-layer** variant, on JLCPCB's
`JLC08161H-7628` (1.6 mm nominal, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, ENIG). The build is uniform:
four 7628 prepreg layers of 0.2104 mm (er 4.4) alternating with three 0.2 mm cores
(er 4.36), 1.6028 mm in all.

The `F.Cu -> In1.Cu` prepreg is **identical to the 4-layer `JLC04161H-7628`**, which is why
the RF geometry carries over unchanged (see *Impedance* below).

| # | Layer | Type | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F.Cu | signal | parts (all on top), signals, 50 ohm RF microstrip, GND pour (kept out of the RF corridor) |
| 2 | In1.Cu | plane | **solid GND** -- the reference for the RF lines and USB |
| 3 | In2.Cu | signal | BGA escape and general routing |
| 4 | In3.Cu | plane | **+3V3** |
| 5 | In4.Cu | plane | **GND** |
| 6 | In5.Cu | signal | BGA escape and general routing |
| 7 | In6.Cu | plane | **GND** |
| 8 | B.Cu | signal | signals, the VSYS_SW trunk, GND pour |

Four routing layers instead of two, and every one of them sits against a ground plane.
The planes carry no traces. In the Specctra DSN the four plane layers are typed `power`,
which is what keeps Freerouting off them.

**Rules** (also in `steering_wheel.kicad_pro`, see `tools/board_rules.py`):
- Clearance 0.1 mm, tracks >= 0.1 mm (signals 0.127 mm).
- Vias 0.4/0.2 mm; BGA fan-out vias **0.35/0.2 mm**; stitching vias 0.45/0.2 mm.
- Copper to edge 0.3 mm, hole to hole 0.25 mm, minimum annular ring 0.075 mm.

**Via drill: 0.2 mm, not 0.15 mm.** The 4-layer board used a 0.3/0.15 mm BGA via. Through
1.6 mm that is a **10.7:1** aspect ratio, and JLCPCB's published limit is **10:1 maximum
with 8:1 recommended**, so it is out of spec on an 8-layer board. A 0.2 mm drill gives
**8.01:1**. The pad grows to 0.35 mm, which is also JLC's preferred +0.15 mm over the hole.
The fan-out re-solved with the larger padstack and still placed all **77 vias / 84
dogbones**, i.e. no ball lost its dogbone site.

The alternative would have been a 1.2 mm board (`JLC08121H-3313`, 8:1 with the old
0.15 mm drill), but its F.Cu -> In1.Cu prepreg is only 0.0994 mm, which would have pushed
the 50 ohm width down to ~0.17 mm and forced a rework of the whole tuned RF corridor.

**Impedance.** Checked against this stackup (Hammerstad-Jensen microstrip with copper
thickness and solder-mask correction):

| Line | Geometry | Result |
|---|---|---|
| RF 50 ohm, F.Cu over In1 | w = 0.34 mm, h = 0.2104 mm, er 4.4 | **51.7 ohm** -- within 5 %, unchanged from the 4-layer board |
| USB 2.0 pair, F.Cu over In1 | w = 0.2 mm, gap 0.15 mm | **~128 ohm differential** -- see below |

**Open item: the USB pair is not 90 ohm.** USB 2.0 wants 90 ohm +/-15 % differential; the
`USB` net class (0.2 mm / 0.15 mm gap) gives about 128 ohm on this stackup. Reaching 90 ohm
needs **~0.40 mm** traces at a 0.15 mm gap, widened in the run and left narrow only at the
USB-C pads, where 0.4 mm will not fit between the pins. This is **not** a consequence of
the 8-layer conversion: the F.Cu/In1.Cu geometry is the same on the 4-layer board, so the
4-layer design has the same ~128 ohm. It was left as-is here so this variant stays a pure
layer-count change; decide it once for both boards. The link is short and the RW612's USB
is USB 2.0, so full-speed traffic is unaffected; it matters for high-speed.

**Floorplan** (board coordinates: origin at the top-left of the outline's bounding box, y down; mirror axis x = 69.45):
- **LCD module** (108 × 60.88 mm, on User.Drawings) is centred on the axis at y = 12.8–73.7. That leaves a ~10 mm strip above it, and its bottom edge sits 0.5 mm inside the lowest point of the bottom edge. The module connects by cable to J501, so it no longer has to sit on the board; the outline marks where it was assumed to be. Only low parts go underneath: the tallest are J103 (4.75 mm), the USB-C (3.2 mm) and the 2 mm inductors. **The DXF doesn't show the display window, so check this position against the faceplate.**
- **Side wings, outside the LCD:** all 12 control connectors, in two columns of three per side, pin 1 at the bottom.
  - Left: ABTN1–3 (outer column), ABTN4, ENC1, BTN1 (inner column).
  - Right: ABTN5–7, ABTN8, ENC2, BTN2.
  - The expansion header J515 is at the top of the left wing.
  - The NeoPixel connector J502 and its 220 µF cap C504 are at the top of the right wing. The level shifter U501 is just inside the LCD corner.
- **Strip above the LCD:**
  - Left: the LCD cable connector J501 (14-pin JST PH), on the side where the module has its header.
  - Centre: the U.FL on the top bump.
  - Right: SWD J201, RESET, ISP, UART J202 and the user LED.
  - The debug connectors stay reachable with the display fitted.
- **RW612, RF front end, flash, PSRAM, crystals, core-buck parts:** the hand-tuned block of rev 0.1, moved as one rigid group (`CORE` offset in `layout_steering.py`). The RF corridor and all its pre-routes are unchanged.
- **Power section:** the rev 0.1 floorplan and its hand-routed switcher loops, moved as one rigid group (`PWR` offset) so the USB-C sits in the bottom-centre bump.
  - The battery connector J103 now faces the bottom-left ear, clear of Q101 and the screw.
  - The power switch SW101 sits on the bottom edge, rotated about 9° to follow it, with the actuator flush with the edge.
  - The optional NTC header J102 is next to J103.
- **TCA9555 (U502) and fuse F501:** under the LCD, right of centre.

**Status: placed, not routed.** This copy of the design carries the schematic and the
placement only. The locked copper that belongs to the floorplan is included (power loops,

## 7. Check before ordering

1. **Project footprints** (`lib/steering_wheel.pretty`) use nominal land patterns. Verify each against the vendor drawings:
   - RW612 TFBGA145 (NXP SOT2153-1): 0.25 mm pads, +0.05 mm mask.
   - SKY13323-378LF (6-pin MLPD 1×1 mm, 0.35 mm pitch assumed).
   - **TDK DPX165950DT pad numbering** (1 GND, 2 COM, 3 GND, 4 HB, 5 GND, 6 LB) and pads (0.30×0.60 mm, 0.55 mm pitch).
2. **LCD cable.** J501 pin *n* goes to module pin *n* (1 VCC, 2 GND, 3 CS, 4 RST, 5 DC, 6 MOSI, 7 SCK, 8 LED, 9 MISO, 10 SCL, 11 CTP_RST, 12 SDA, 13 INT, 14 SD_CS).
   - Board end: JST PHR-14 housing.
   - Module end: the module has 2.54 mm male pins, so use a 2.54 mm female housing, or solder the wires.
   - The 14 wires share one GND. Keep the cable short (about 15 cm or less) and start with a moderate SPI clock (about 20 MHz), raising it once the display is stable.
3. **Mechanics against the wheel** (the DXF has only the outline and holes):
   - the LCD position (centred, y = 12.8–73.7) against the faceplate window;
   - USB-C at the bottom-centre bump, power switch on the bottom edge left of it, U.FL at the top-centre bump;
   - the wing connectors, and the mated JST PH height (about 8 mm above the board);
   - the 3.0 mm holes: an M3 screw normally wants 3.2–3.4 mm.
4. MAX17048 TDFN exposed-pad size vs the `TDFN-8-1EP_2x2mm_P0.5mm_EP0.8x1.2mm` footprint.
5. **Battery polarity**: J103 pin 1 = BAT+. JST-PH LiPo packs are not consistent, so check yours.
6. **Fab options (8-layer):** order as **8 layers, 1.6 mm, stackup `JLC08161H-7628`** (1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, ENIG). The smallest drill is **0.2 mm** (BGA fan-out, 0.35 mm pad), an 8:1 aspect ratio, so the 0.15 mm small-via option is **no longer needed**. There is no via-in-pad and all vias are tented. Confirm with JLC: that they will build `JLC08161H-7628` as specified (their public impedance table only lists 4- and 6-layer stackups, though their API carries the 8-layer templates and their capability page offers impedance control for 8 layers at +/-10 %), and ask for impedance control on the RF lines if you want it. Get the 8-layer price before committing -- it is the main cost difference against the 4-layer variant.
8. **Q101 (FS8205A) pads 1 and 8.** The symbol has a `D12` pin (the common drain) that the
   schematic leaves unconnected, and the footprint puts it on pads 1 and 8. KiCad gives
   both pads the same auto net and DRC therefore reports one unconnected item on every
   build of this design, 4-layer and 8-layer alike. Check the FS8205A pinout against the
   datasheet before ordering: on the usual SOP-8 the pins are 1,2,3 = S1 / 4 = G1 /
   5 = G2 / 6,7,8 = S2, which would make pads 1 and 8 part of BAT_N and GND rather than a
   separate drain pin. If that is right, the protection FET is running on two of three
   source pads per side (higher Rds(on), still functional) and the symbol/footprint pin
   map wants fixing in `steering_wheel.py` + `tools/fpgen.py`. Nothing on the board can fix
   it, so it was left alone here.
9. DPX165950DT-8085D1 and RW612 are not stocked at LCSC. Source them from DigiKey/Mouser/NXP, or ask JLC for a global-sourcing quote.

## 10. Schematic review (rev 0.2, 2026-09-30)

What was run: SKiDL ERC, `check_sch.py`, KiCad 9 ERC on all sheets, and KiCad's netlist compared with SKiDL's (all clean, see section 5). Then every IC and interface was checked by hand against its datasheet and the review checklist.

**Must-fix:** none found.

**Should-fix**
1. **Charge LEDs back-power the RW612 when the board is switched off on battery.**
   - The path: VSYS → R108/R109 (1 k) → D101/D102 → R110/R111 (10 k) → GPIO56/55 clamp diodes and R112/R113 (100 k) into the unpowered +3V3 rail.
   - While ~PGOOD/~CHG are open (no USB), roughly 0.1–0.2 mA flows per LED. The orange "charging" LED glows dimly while off, the battery drains, and the MCU's I/O rail is partly powered.
   - Fix: replace R110/R111 with small Schottky diodes (BAT54-type, cathode at the BQ24074 pin). The MCU still reads low when the charger pulls the pin low, and nothing flows when +3V3 is off.
2. **No ESD protection on the 12 control connectors.** The lines go to buttons the driver touches, with only series R plus RC in front of the TCA9555 and RW612 pins. Add TVS arrays at the connectors (e.g. 4-channel TPD4E05U06-class), including VCC_BTN.
3. **Touch pull-ups R507–R510 (to +3V3) feed the LCD module whenever BOOST_EN is low**, about 1–2 mA through the FT6336U pin clamps. Either keep BOOST_EN high whenever the RW612 runs (firmware), or run the module from 3.3 V (fit R502 instead of R501) if it works at that voltage.

**Notes**
- **LCD cable:** SPI, the touch I2C, reset, the backlight and the LCD supply return through a single GND wire (the module's header has only one). The 22 Ω series resistors on SCK/MOSI help. Keep the cable short, and if it has to be long, twist SCK with GND.
- **TCA9555 at power-up:** all pins are inputs with pull-ups, so the *in* lines float weakly high until firmware sets port 0 to outputs (section 4).
- **F501 (0.2 A hold) budget:** it covers all eight buttons together. Check the buttons' LED current.
- **USB input limit:** ILIM 1.42 A is above what a plain USB 2.0 port gives (0.5 A). The BQ24074's input DPM backs off when VBUS sags, and a Type-C charger is fine.
- **Gauge accuracy:** the MAX17048 sees the cell through the protection FETs in the ground path, so there are a few tens of mV of error while charging at 1 A. That's fine for state of charge.
- **Charger heat:** the BQ24074 dissipates up to ~1.5–2 W charging a flat cell at 1 A, and thermal regulation will reduce the current. Give its exposed pad a via array to the GND planes and some copper.
- **Test points:** there are none for +1V8, VCORE or VCC_BTN (probe at their capacitors).

**Checked and fine**
- **USB-C:** 5.1 k Rd on both CC pins, USBLC6 ESD at the connector, 10 µF on VBUS (the USB maximum), 1 k into RW612 USB_VBUS.
- **BQ24074:**
  - ISET 887 Ω → 1.0 A, ILIM 1.13 k → 1.42 A, TMR 68 k → about 9 h.
  - TS 10 k bias.
  - EN1 = 0 / EN2 = 1 (current limit from ILIM), ~CE low, EP to GND.
- **DW01A + FS8205A:** the reference circuit, with 100 Ω / 100 nF / 1 k.
- **AO3401A switch:** the body diode blocks VSYS_SW → VSYS, with a 100 k gate pull-up.
- **TPS63001:** PS/SYNC low (power save), 2.2 µH, 2 × 22 µF out, VINA RC.
- **TPS61023:** FB 732 k / 100 k → 4.99 V, 1 µH, 2 × 22 µF.
- **NeoPixel:** AHCT buffer on 5 V (3.3 V in → 5 V out), 330 Ω series, 220 µF bulk.
- **I2C:**
  - PM bus: gauge 0x36, expander 0x20 and the expansion header, with one pull-up pair (4.7 k).
  - Touch 0x38 on its own bus.
  - Every open-drain line (~ALRT, ~INT, ~PGOOD, ~CHG) has a pull-up.
- **Encoder and button inputs:** pull-ups and RC filters as in the Alps application note.
- **Debug and boot:** SWD and UART exposed; RESET RC; ISP and RF_CNTL2 straps as on the FRDM.
- **RW612 balls:** the 22 unconnected balls are spare GPIOs (with GPIO6–8 kept free for FC1 SPI), USB_ID, RF_CNTL3 and the boot straps with internal defaults.
